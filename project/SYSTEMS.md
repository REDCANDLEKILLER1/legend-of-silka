# THE LEGEND OF SILKA — Systems: Health, Magic, Growth

Status: PROPOSAL with rulings applied — Remy's draft, 2026-11-26,
at Michael's direction ("build the health and magic... everything
Zelda II has, fused with Zelda I"). Michael's rulings of
2026-11-26 are IN and marked: **hearts, not blocks; no lives;
3 hearts to start; pieces and whole containers; saving by
milestone save points and by pausing.** The rest is Michael's to
approve, cut, or rename. Story lives in SCRIPT.md; this doc is
the machine under it.

## The fusion, in one line

Zelda II's systems — life and magic meters, spells taught by
people, experience that levels three tracks, side-view combat with
a high/low guard — carried on Zelda I's bones: the top-down
overworld, heart-style growth, and the item-get moment.

## Health — hearts (Michael's ruling, 2026-11-26)

- The HUD carries **hearts**, Zelda I style — not Zelda II's
  blocks. CornMan starts with **3 Heart Containers**. The hearts
  are drawn in **#00FF00** (Michael's ruling, 2026-11-26) — the
  house green on black, against the magic meter's gold: green is
  his life, gold is the kingdom's magic.
- His maximum grows two ways, both found, never bought:
  **Pieces of Heart** (four pieces make a container) hidden
  through the world, and **whole Heart Containers** resting in
  special places — the heart of a palace, the end of a great deed.
- Damage is a **table, not a formula** (Zelda II's true design,
  verified in RESEARCH-ZELDA-HEALTH.md): every enemy carries a
  damage class, and a hit reads class × his LIFE level off a
  table, paid in fractions of a heart. The weakest class falls
  from a full heart at LIFE 1 to a quarter-heart at LIFE 8; the
  top class is the instant-death class at *every* level. **The
  Wizard's purple fire is that class.** It never gets cheaper, at
  any level, ever.
- **No lives.** There is no life counter, no dolls, no game-over
  march back to the palace. Death is handled by the save system
  below: he wakes at his last save, whole — what he earned since
  that save is lost with the time, and that loss is the sting.

## Saving (Michael's ruling, 2026-11-26)

- **Milestone save points:** after each milestone — a scene
  turned, a quest finished, the wrench earned, an Emerald made
  safe — the game sets a save point. Fall, and he wakes there.
- **Pause and save:** the player may pause anywhere and save.
  Two doors to safety: the story's milestones, and the player's
  own hand.
- **Sleeping in a bed saves** (Michael's addition, 2026-11-26):
  a bed is the in-world save. His own bed at the cottage, a bed
  at Winnie's inn, a bed in any friendly house — sleeping saves
  the game **and restores hearts and magic in full**. The split
  that keeps every door honest: pause-save saves progress only;
  a bed saves *and* restores, so rest stays a place you travel
  to, not a menu you open. The bed was already this story's
  hinge — the dream leaves him in one, and he wakes in his own.

## Magic

- A MAGIC **bar** sits under the hearts, golden — gold is the
  kingdom's magic color, the same gold as the pit chains' glow.
  Starts at 4 blocks; **Magic Containers** found in the world
  raise it to 8 (Zelda II's measure, kept for magic only).
- **The fusion, drawn** (Michael's screenshots, 2026-11-26): the
  BAR is Zelda II's — magic gets the bar. The HEARTS are Zelda
  I's — life gets the hearts. Two different games, one HUD. The
  bar carries Zelda II's HUD language with it: the track levels
  shown beside their meters, and **NEXT** — the experience still
  needed for the next level-up — counted at the top.
- Every spell costs magic; the MAGIC level lowers the cost.
- Restoration is **people, split in two** (Zelda II's town law):
  Winnie restores LIFE at the inn; Father Corin restores MAGIC at
  the temple. Rest is a place and a person, not a menu.

### Spells — taught by people, never found in a box

Zelda II's law: a wise man in a town teaches each spell, some only
after a kindness is done. Ours are taught by the cast he already
knows:

| Spell | What it does | Teacher |
|---|---|---|
| MEND | Restores exactly 3 hearts — healing is rationed, never a full heal (Zelda II's LIFE) | Winnie, at the inn |
| HUSK | A golden husk of light; halves all damage until it breaks | Father Corin, at the temple |
| EMBER | Golden fire from the wrench's head (Zelda II's FIRE) | Brand, at the forge |
| LEAP | A jump twice his height, for side-view heights (Zelda II's JUMP) | Vesper, at the tower |
| TURN | The wrench turned flat reflects spells back at their caster (Zelda II's REFLECT) | Father Corin, later |
| THUNDER | The sky answers: every enemy on screen falls. Costly. Late game (Zelda II's THUNDER) | OPEN |

FAIRY and SPELL from Zelda II are held in reserve — no Silka form
proposed yet.

## Techniques — free, taught, no magic cost

- **SPIN** — the warden's spin, taught by the garrison captain in
  the courtyard during the Day, before the descent (the wrench
  quest). The answer to being surrounded.
- **DOWN SLAM** — in side view, strike downward in the air onto
  what is below (Zelda II's Down Thrust). Teacher: OPEN (a guard
  in Part Two's village?).
- **UP THRUST** — strike upward at what hangs or flies above.
  Teacher: OPEN.

## Growth — experience, three tracks

Enemies and deeds earn experience. At each threshold he chooses
one track to raise (Zelda II's law — the choice is the player's),
or **cancels** and keeps banking toward a dearer track:

- **ATTACK** — the wrench hits harder.
- **MAGIC** — every spell costs less.
- **LIFE** — every hit he takes costs him less.

Two mercies ride on the system (both verified Zelda II law):
**raising LIFE refills the life meter to full, and raising MAGIC
refills the magic meter** — a level chosen at the right moment is
a potion. And **securing a palace's Emerald tops his experience
up to the next threshold automatically** — Zelda II's crystal
rule wearing our canon: every Emerald made safe guarantees a
level.

## Combat shape (side view, Zelda II)

- The wrench strikes where he aims: high or low. His guard is the
  same — he can block high or low with the wrench's flat; what he
  cannot do is block both at once.
- The spin covers all around him but leaves him open the moment
  after — power with a price, or it is the only move anyone uses.
