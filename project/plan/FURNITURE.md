# Furniture craft — Michael's mission, 2026-11-20

"Learn how to make furniture." Rules, from the ALttP reference standard
already on file (characters/reference-alttp-npcs/ + the Kakariko set):

1. Furniture is drawn at the same detail standard as people: 3+ tones per
   material, one light (upper left), cast shadow on the floor side,
   per-piece variation. A flat rectangle with legs is a tile, not furniture.
2. Furniture has a FRONT. Beds show headboard + pillow + blanket fold;
   thrones show posts, finials, cushion, crest; tables show the top plane
   AND the front edge (the 45° camera sees both).
3. Signature pieces are PROPS, not tiles: the throne is one composed image
   (throne + seated King) drawn as an entity, so it can be taller than a
   tile and centred on its space. Tiles are for repeated pieces (beds,
   chairs, tables); props are for the one-of-one.
4. Every piece answers: whose is it? The King's bed is not the guest bed:
   gold finials and the cob crest say whose. The Queen's: plum and a mirror.
5. Furniture never blocks a walkway invisibly. If a passage is gated, it
   gets a DOOR — open, or locked with a line that says so.

Built under this standard: the Throne of the Harvest Kings (castle +
feast hall), the grand beds and the Queen's vanity (2F), on top of the
existing tables, shelves, armchairs, hearths and barrels.
