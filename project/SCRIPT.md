## The shape of the whole game

Part One opens with a dream: CornMan falls into a village he has never
seen, and strangers carry him to their inn. He wakes in his own bed at
the castle — it was only a dream — and plays out his last normal day.
That night the attack makes the dream start coming true, and the King
is taken. Part Two opens with the fall again — this time for real, and
this time the player plays the village the dream only showed him.

The dream is not a trick and not a bad omen hanging over the game. It
is a warning he doesn't understand yet. When he falls for real, the
player already knows every face in that village — and none of them
know him. He dreamed them before he ever met them.

## PART ONE — THE DREAM AND THE DAY

### Scene 1 — The Dream (the fall)

*Night. A storm over a village CornMan has never seen. Something falls
out of the sky — CornMan — down the village path. The inn lady and a
soldier find him where he landed.*

- SOLDIER: "They're coming!"
- INN LADY: "Who is this?"
- SOLDIER: "I don't know."
- INN LADY: "We have to take him in!"
- INN LADY: "Quick! To the inn!"

*They pick him up and carry him through the rain to the inn.*

### Scene 3 — The Day

*Colonel Corn's normal day — the last one. He is King Cob's Kernel,
and tomorrow is the festival. The player walks the castle and the
village, meets everyone in their regular parts, and learns who
CornMan is by living his day: who he is to the King, to the guards,
to the village.*

The village cast is introduced here in their everyday lives — the
inn lady at her inn, the soldier at his post, the townspeople at
their work. Regular parts only. Nothing burns yet. The player has
seen these faces before — in the dream — and that is the point.

> Status: the castle and village maps are built and playable; the

### Scene 4 — The Night It Starts

*Midnight. A calm night — water moving, lamps flickering. Then a
large BOOM, and screams. The village is burning.*

CornMan runs through the burning village to the palace — the run
plays on the village map — and the confrontation plays inside, on the
castle-interior map, where the guards are already down.

The Storm Wizard stands before King Cob. He has come for the Corn
Emerald.

## PART TWO — THE DREAM COMES TRUE

### Scene 5 — The Fall, For Real

Part Two opens exactly where the dream opened: the storm, the
village, CornMan falling out of the sky. The player has watched this
once. This time it is real, and this time he lands on his feet in a
village under attack — and the game hands him the controls.

### Scene 6 — The Village, For Real

The cast he dreamed is here, in their regular parts — the inn lady,
the soldier, the townspeople — except now their home is burning, and
their bigger parts begin: **the villagers fight the fires.** Buckets
from the well and the river, carried to the doors — the nearest free
hands take the nearest fire. CornMan fights beside people who have
never seen him before, in a village he already knows by heart.

From here the quest opens: every king and every place holds an
Emerald, and the Storm Wizard is gathering them all for the Evil
King. If he gets them all, they will rule. CornMan goes to war for
the Corn Emerald, for King Cob, and for Princess Silka.

> version). The firefighting system is designed (nearest-free-hands
> trigger system); the scenes are to be written here as we build.

## The cast

- **CornMan** — the hero. King Cob's Kernel ("Colonel Corn"). His
  weapon is a wrench. The C on his chest is his emblem — he carries
  no shield.
- **King Cob** — King of the corn kingdom. Kidnapped in Part One.
  Holder of the Corn Emerald.
- **Princess Silka** — her golden hair is corn silk. (Her scenes are
  to be written.)
- **The Storm Wizard** — faceless pointed hood, red eyes, purple
  fire. Gathering the Emeralds for the Evil King.
- **The Evil King** — the power behind the Wizard. Offstage so far.
- **The Inn Lady** — keeps the village inn. In the dream she takes
  CornMan in; in the real village she is the heart of it.
- **The Soldier** — village guard. First to reach the fall, in the
  dream and in truth.
- **The villagers** — the village cast. Regular parts now; the
  firefighting is theirs when the fire night comes.

Names for the inn lady, the soldier, and the villagers: not set yet.

## Standing facts (canon — do not contradict)

- Every king and every place holds an **Emerald**. The Storm Wizard
  gathers them for the **Evil King**. If he gets them all, they rule.
- The Wizard's fire is **purple**.
- CornMan's house is the west cottage by the mill, in the village.
  His room at the castle is where Part One's waking happens.
- The castle: grand stairs reach only their own floor's balcony;
  floors connect only through the tower staircases; the castle exit
  is the ground-floor main doors only. There is no door out of the
  second floor.
- The dream (Scene 1) and the fall (Scene 5) are the same event,
  seen twice — once dreamed, once real.
