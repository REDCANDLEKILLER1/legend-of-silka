THE JOB
Swap the castle's ground and upper plates for the two new plates, re-measure the walk data (walls, doors, link spots) to the new art, and rewire the stairs and towers by the three rules below. The plates are finished art — do not redraw, patch, or "improve" them; if a plate seems to fight the wiring, report it instead of editing the art.

THE THREE RULES
1. The big stairs on each floor go only to that floor's own balcony. They never change floors and never leave the room. (All four grand-stair floor-change links come out; the flights land on the same floor's balcony landing.)
2. The only way to change floors is the staircase inside the towers, entered at the tower bases on the ground floor (north-west and north-east towers) and arriving through the tower doors on the second floor.
3. The castle's only entrance/exit is the ground floor's main doors. THE SECOND FLOOR HAS NO DOOR — none drawn, none wired, no exit of any kind from that floor. (Michael's ruling, 2026-11-24.)

WHAT THE NEW ART ALREADY HAS (verify, don't redo)

- Second floor: the gallery stairs run open onto their balcony; the south wall is plain — no door; the towers stay as drawn.

ACCEPTANCE (walk it — don't spot-check)
- Enter the castle from the village; the entry trigger works as it does in the base build.
- Up any grand stair, you arrive on the SAME floor's balcony, and you can walk back down.
- Ground floor to second floor ONLY through a tower staircase, both directions.
- No way out of the castle from the second floor; leaving works only at the ground floor's main doors, back to the village.
- Every door drawn on the new plates opens to the room it belongs to; no walk-through walls anywhere on either floor.
