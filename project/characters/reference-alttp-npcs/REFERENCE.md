## The detail standard these set (Michael: "a lot more detail than you realize")
- Every NPC is a distinct silhouette AND a distinct palette — innkeeper,
  informant, shopkeeper, sage read apart at a glance, across a room.
- 3-4 tones per surface: highlight, mid, shade, dark outline — never a
  flat fill. Hair has volume and lit strands; beards have texture.
- Faces carry features: eyes with whites, noses, mustaches — at 16px.
- Clothes are tailored: folds, trim, belts, layered robes (the King of
  Hyrule: green/gold layered robe, crown, scepter in hand).
- One identifying prop or trait per person: the King's scepter, the
  Hermit's tree-disguise, the Fortune Teller's hood, Kiki's cap.
- Outlines are dark but selective — colored shading does most of the
  separation work, not a black box around everything.
