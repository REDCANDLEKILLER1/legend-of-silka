# CASTLE-FOOT VILLAGE — PLAN (2026-11-19, planning chat)

The village below the castle. Where old Pearl lives, where the player
starts (Beat 9 handoff), and the game's first home ground.

## The jobs it has to do
1. START — Corn Man walks in here on the village-morning music.
2. THE TELLING — old Pearl's cottage is the frame of the whole game:
   the player goes to her first; the castle story opens from her door.
3. SAFE HAVEN — rest/heal/save ground (the standing village rule:
   nothing attacks here, ever).
4. FIRST LESSONS — movement, talking, doors, a sign board; gentle,
   no combat. (Does not repeat Eli's road lessons — separate question.)
5. FIRST SHOP — supplies + the blacksmith's first upgrade path.
6. THE VIEW — a Ruin Overlook at the north edge: the ruined castle
   above, visible from the street. The wound stays in sight.
7. THE ROAD OUT — one south road to the game's first field.

## Footprint & anchors (adopts Google's harness coordinates, already tested)
- 32x24 tiles (2 wide x 2 tall screens), castle approach to the north.
- Spawn (15,21) south-center · Old Pearl (7,6) northwest ·
  Ruin Overlook (15,2) north edge. All three already walk-verified
  against the fixed harness logic.

## What's in it
- Old Pearl's cottage (NW) — story anchor; her door is the game's first door.
- Village square (center) — the marble fountain and the Harvest King's
  golden statue, BOTH reused from the castle courtyard pack: the same
  pieces a hundred harvests later, weathered. Rose planters, benches.
- Inn — rest/save, feast-day music's descendant (a fiddler by the fire).
- General store — first shop.
- Blacksmith — Stonecutter clan; first weapon path.
- Houses x4-5 — Miller, Orchard, Stonecutter clans (Google's recolor
  system: the feast folk's descendants, zero new base designs).
- Corn fields (south/west edges), orchard (east), mill on the field road.
- Well, fences, pig pen, notice board by the square.
- Small chapel — a quiet place, seeds not sermons. (Michael's call.)

## Who's in it (~12 NPCs, mostly from existing designs)
Old Pearl · shopkeep · smith · innkeep · one elder per clan (3) ·
two mill/orchard hands · two children · a traveling crow-keeper?
(NO — crows belong to the storm; keep them out of the village's peace.)

## Michael's decisions — RULED 2026-11-19 00:11 CST
1. Size: KEEP 32x24. Tight on purpose — south gate to overlook in
   under 20 seconds, the ruin always looming, no filler.
2. The ruin: LOOK-ONLY in Act 1. Balustrade at the overlook; the
   silhouette above the cliff. Nobody climbs until later.
3. Heal & save: BOTH. Pearl's hearth is free (true home base); the
   inn by the south road serves travelers on arrival.
4. Chapel: IN. Northeast corner of the park — the valley's reverence
   for the Harvest King and the Great Barn.
