# KAKARIKO REFERENCE — Michael's screenshots (filed 2026-11-19 00:19 CST)

His chosen standard: ALttP Kakariko Village + Minish Cap Hyrule Town.
Grammar only — patterns and proportions. Nintendo's art itself is
never shipped (standing rule from the z2disassembly study).

## What the real maps actually do (distilled from the shots)
1. GRASS FIRST. The ground is grass; dirt paths are sparse and only
   connect doors to routes. Most of the map is unpaved green.
2. TREES COME IN MASSES. Overlapping round crowns form one forest
   mass (NW Kakariko) — never single trees dotted on a grid. The
   tended version is the opposite read: the orchard is a neat
   plantation grid. Wild mass vs tended grid, nothing in between.
3. FEW HOUSES, BIG YARDS. ~7 buildings on the whole Kakariko map.
   The richness is in yards: log-post fences, hedge borders, dirt
   patches, troughs, pots — each house owns its ground.
4. TERRACES ARE BROWN AND DEEP. Cliff bands carry a grass lip on
   top and striated earth below; stairs are cut stone; a cave mouth
   sits IN the band, not beside it.
5. THE PLAZA IS ONE STONE SQUARE. Weathervane statue center-north,
   hedge U opening toward the player, compass letters on dirt.
6. MICRO-PROPS ARE SPARSE AND PLACED. 4-pack clay pots on the
   vendor's dirt mat, a signpost by a door, paired stone welcome
   mats under thresholds, tiny flower dots in grass — few, deliberate.
7. ROOFS ARE THE COLOR. One dominant green world; the roofs
   (maroon, blue) are the accents that let you navigate by color.

## Gaps vs our Kakariko-grade pack (fold in at build time)
- Our '+' paving is too generous — cut paths back to dirt spurs
  serving doors; let grass take the rest.
- Our trees are single tiles — build 2x2/3x3 canopy-cluster tiles
  for wild masses; keep single trees only in the orchard grid.
- More air: our houses sit closer than Kakariko's. The 48x36 has
  room to give each building its yard if paths shrink.
- Everything else in the pack (terraces, stairs, cave mouth, hedge
  park, vendor mat, welcome mats, signposts) already matches.
